Getting here

  • Train: Paisley Gilmour Street is a six minute walk to Abbey Close.
  • Bus: services along Gauze Street stop at Paisley Town Hall.
  • Car: the Anchor Mills and Lagoon car parks are a ten minute walk. There is no parking on Abbey Close during the market.
  • Accessible parking: four bays on Cotton Street, booked through the market office.

Good to know

  • Well behaved dogs are welcome in the outdoor village.
  • Card payments are accepted by most traders; the site has two cash machines.
  • Quiet hour is Sunday 10:00 to 11:00, with the stage sound turned down.
  • Lost property is held at the market office in the Town Hall.
