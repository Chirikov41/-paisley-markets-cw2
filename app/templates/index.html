Trading with us

Applications are reviewed by the market office. Pitches are allocated by category to keep the village balanced.

  1. Prepare your details

    You will need a contact name, the category that fits your stall and a short description of what you sell.

  2. Submit the application

    It takes about five minutes. You receive a reference such as PCM-{{ market.year }}-0007 straight away.

  3. Office review

    Applications are checked within ten working days for category fit, food safety paperwork and power requirements.

  4. Offer and confirmation

    Approved traders receive a pitch offer, a licence pack and an invoice for the pitch fee.
