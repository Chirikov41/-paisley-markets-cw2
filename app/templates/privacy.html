Privacy notice

How we handle trader applications

This notice explains what the market office collects when a trader applies for a pitch, why it is needed and how long it is kept.

Who is responsible for the data

The market office acts as data controller for the application form. Contact the office at {{ market.contact_email }} for any question about this notice.

What we collect

  • Business name and the name of the person applying.
  • A contact email address and, optionally, a telephone number.
  • The category of goods, a description of what you sell and the weekends you want to trade.
  • The pitch type and whether you need an electrical supply.
  • The date and time you gave consent, kept as evidence that consent was given.

We do not ask for date of birth, identity documents, bank details or any special category data.

Why we need it, and our lawful basis

We use the data to assess the application, to allocate pitches by category and to contact you about the offer, the licence pack and the pitch fee. The lawful basis is consent, which you give by ticking the declaration on the form. You can withdraw consent at any time; we will then stop processing the application and delete it unless we are required to keep it for legal reasons.

How long we keep it

Applications are kept for 12 months after the market closes, so that we can answer queries about pitch allocation and repeat traders. After that the record is deleted. Rejected applications are kept for 12 months as well, because traders often ask why a category was full.

Who else sees it

Only the market office staff who review applications can see the full record. A trading officer may see the business name and category for pitch planning. We do not sell data and we do not use it for marketing. The site is hosted on a platform that stores the database, and the hosting provider processes the data on our instructions.

Your rights

  • Access: the status page shows the record we hold for your reference and email address.
  • Rectification: ask the office to correct a mistake and it is amended on the same record.
  • Erasure: the office can delete an application permanently, and the deletion is written to an audit log.
  • Portability: the office can export your application as a CSV file on request.
  • Complaint: you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with our response.

How the data is protected

The application form is served over HTTPS in deployment, sessions are signed with a server side secret, every form post carries a cross site request forgery token, and office passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes rather than plain text. Administrative actions are written to an audit log with the time, the actor and the record affected.

Cookies and tracking

The public pages set one session cookie, which holds the security token used by forms. No analytics, advertising or third party tracking scripts are loaded.
